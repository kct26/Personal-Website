Add company/school logo images to this folder to replace the plain
black initials badges (like "DU" or "CO") with real logos.

Based on src/data/content.ts, this project already expects:

    public/logos/drexel.jpg
    public/logos/lhp.jpg

The "logo" field on each experience[]/education[] entry in content.ts
already points at these paths — just drop matching image files in here
and refresh. Any reasonably-sized image works; the badge automatically
shows the whole logo uncropped and centered (object-fit: contain), so
you don't need to pre-scale or crop anything.

If an entry has no "logo" field (or you delete the line in content.ts),
it falls back to the plain initials badge automatically.

You can delete this .txt file once you've added your logos — it's just
a placeholder marker, not read by the app.
